# FarmRPG Toolkit: features in detail

Every feature, and each part of it, can be switched on or off on the **FarmRPG Toolkit** settings page
(**My Settings > FarmRPG Toolkit**, or **Settings** in the Toolkit menu): each feature's switch is on its row, and a
tap on its name shows its options (folded on phones, open on wider screens; "Open all" / "Fold all" at the top).
Each feature below has the same parts:

- **Where:** the game pages it adds to.
- **What you see:** what it adds there.
- **Settings:** its options on the settings page, as they're labelled there (all on unless marked *off*).
- **Data:** where its facts and numbers come from.

## Friendship Gifts

**Where:** Friendship Levels, townsfolk mailboxes, item pages.

**What you see:**
- **Friendship Levels:** each townsfolk's Super Loves, Loves, Likes and Hates as pictures.
  - Loves and likes you've already given are faded; a row where you've given everything is hidden.
  - Tap Loves, Likes or Hates to fold that row on every card (it stays folded).
  - Townsfolk of the Day has a gold outline and the XP bonus it gives.
  - Four townsfolk per row on wide screens.
- **A townsfolk's mailbox:** the XP each item gives (Townsfolk of the Day, Over The Moon, event bonuses and your perks
  included), the level it would take you to, the best gifts first, and the loves and likes you haven't given and
  have none of.
- **Item pages:** which townsfolk love, like or hate the item.

**Settings:**
- Loves, likes and hates on Friendship Levels
- Four townsfolk per row on Friendship Levels on wide screens (phones keep three)
- XP, level preview and best-first sorting in mailboxes
- On item pages: which townsfolk love, like or hate it
- Fade loves and likes you've already given; perks used for XP (found from your Perks and Farm Supply pages)

**Data:** the Library's Gifts and townsfolk pages. What you've given is read from each townsfolk's mailbox when you
open it, and in the background only when something changed.

## Item Uses

**Where:** item pages, in a card under the item's picture.

**What you see:**
- **What it does:** for Quest Artifacts and Tower Artifacts, in the Library's words.
- **Uses:**
  - Help Requests that ask for it: yours first; ones you've completed only in the "N more" tooltip.
  - Crafts and meals it goes into; a recipe it teaches.
  - Farm buildings that use it, in the game's words: "Used by your Sugar Cane Mill farm building: White Sugar, Brown
    Sugar, Molasses" (what each makes from it), "Used by your Feed Mill farm building: 12 Feed each", and the Wine
    Cellar, Pig Pen, Raptor Pen, Orchard and Grape Juice Vat, each linked to the building on your farm.
  - Wishing Well, Exchange Center, Locksmith (keys), mining tools, places it unlocks.
  - Townsfolk who love or like it.
  - No use anywhere: "none yet: a Collectible", the Library's word for it.
- **Where to get it:** first the farm buildings that produce it, in the game's words, with what goes in, how often,
  the perks and meals that raise it (yours or not) and, when it isn't on your farm, how to unlock it: "Produced by
  your Sugar Cane Mill farm building · from 1 Glass Jar + 5 Unrefined Sugar · instantly", "Produced by your Flour
  Mill farm building · 14.4 from each Wheat · 1 a minute · doubled with your Flour Power". Which buildings you have
  is read when you open your farm. Then drops (exploring, fishing, mining, farming), containers and the open shops
  that sell them, crafting, cooking, shops, the Flea Market, Borgen's Camp (its Ancient Coin price and when it was
  last sold; every week known in the tooltip), Help Request rewards, townsfolk and pet rewards, the Tower, the Temple,
  and more.
- **Museum:** "not in your collection yet", only for items missing from your Museum.
- **More:** links to the item's Library page (when it has one) and its buddy.farm page.

Nothing the game's own page already shows (Givable, Sell Price, Temple of Reward, Cooking Use) is repeated. Tap a
section's name to fold it on every item. Each place links to its page in the game.

**Settings:** none apart from switching the feature on or off.

**Data:** buddy.farm (read the first time you open an item, kept a week) and the Library. Your Museum is read once,
the first time an item page needs it, then kept current from the counts the Toolkit already reads.

## Food Enhancement

**Where:** the Quick Meals panel, meal item pages, My Kitchen.

**What you see:** what each meal really does and how long it lasts, with key words in bold. Meals that last a number
of uses say how many each one adds when you eat another ("50 uses · +50 each").

**Settings:**
- Effect and how long it lasts under each meal in Quick Meals
- "Real effect" note on meal item pages when the game's text is vague
- What the meal you pick in My Kitchen does
- Key words in bold (amounts, multipliers, activities)

**Data:** the Library's Meals page.

---

## Meal Reminders

**Where:** next to the page title on the farm, fishing, exploring, pets, sawmill, market, kitchen, Workshop and
townsfolk mailboxes.

**What you see:** a "Food" button with how many of the page's meals are running ("Food 2/6"), green when all are,
orange when one isn't. Tap it for each meal's effect and time left (uses left for Lemon Seltzer). With the Quick
Meals perk, eat from the list ("Eat · 461"); the game still asks before eating.

**Settings:**
- Show the word "Food" and the count (off: just the coloured dot)
- Eat buttons in the list, if you own the Quick Meals perk (the game asks before eating)

**Data:** what's running, from Home and the Quick Meals panel.

## Locksmith Rewards

**Where:** the Locksmith, containers' item pages, every shop that sells containers.

**What you see:**
- **Locksmith:** totals of the gold and valuable items your containers give (tap one to filter the list), and a line
  under each container saying what the amount in its box gives (red when it's more than you have). Every item is
  listed, on one line: when the names don't fit, it shows pictures and amounts, with each name in its tooltip. An
  item turns red when what the container gives would take you over your cap. Ancient Coins say where opening leaves
  you against your cap ("500 → 1,728 / 5,078").
- **A container's item page:** what all of yours give.
- **Shops:** what a container on sale holds and its worth at Flea Market prices.
- **After opening many Void Bags:** the game's popup of pictures lists each item once, with its name and amount.

**Settings:**
- Totals at the top (tap one to filter the list)
- A line under each container saying what the amount in its box gives (red when it's more than you have)
- What grab bags can give (random picks)
- On a container's item page: what all of yours give
- In shops: what a container on sale holds, and its worth at Flea Market prices
- The Void Bag popup list, and its number of columns

**Data:** buddy.farm (Locksmith contents) and the shops you open.

## Flea Market

**Where:** the Flea Market, Borgen's Camp, Borgen Mercantile.

**What you see:**
- Bundles first, with what's inside and what that would cost bought one by one.
- The hourly items say where else they come from, hardest to get elsewhere first, with drop odds per AP or Large Net
  for your perks; an arrow marks a row moved from the game's order.
- How many of each you have, and after you change the amount, how many you'd have after buying (with a warning
  before your cap).

It never buys anything.

**Settings:**
- Bundles first, with what's inside
- Hourly items: how else to get them, hardest first
- Odds per AP and per Large Net, with your perks (off: per catch or explore)
- How many of each you have (and after buying, once you change the amount)
- The same counts at Borgen's Camp and Borgen Mercantile

**Data:** buddy.farm and the Library (drop odds); your counts are read when the page opens.

## Pet Items

**Where:** the All Pet Items page, with the Inventory Monitor perk.

**What you see:** a "Sort: Over max first" toggle next to the game's filter: the items that would go over your cap
first, every item still shown.

**Settings:** none apart from switching the feature on or off.

**Data:** your counts, read when the page opens and when you come back to it.

## Temple Helper

**Where:** the Temple of Reward and each chain's page.

**What you see:**
- Each chain: how many to finish, how many you have, its next reward; ready and finishable chains first.
- The Secret Chains: certificates done and crops to go.
- On a chain's page: buttons that fill in the amount (Next reward, Finish chain, Max). You still press GO.

**Settings:**
- A line under each chain
- Ready and finishable chains first
- A summary at the top
- Amount buttons on a chain's page

**Data:** the Temple's own pages and your counts.

## Mastery

**Where:** the Workshop, "Things found here" at fishing and exploring spots, the farm, item pages' Crafting Recipe.

**What you see:** your mastery count against the next target ("3.1K / 10K", or "Mega Mastered 1.55M"), coloured by
the game's tier group (its name in the tooltip). An item the game gives no mastery says "No mastery".

**Settings:**
- Workshop
- Things found here
- On the farm (the line under the seed menu; needs Crop Amounts)
- An item page's Crafting Recipe (each ingredient)

**Data:** the Mastery page and item pages when you open them (nothing is fetched).

## Crop Amounts

**Where:** the farm, the Grape Juice Vat, the Feed Mill.

**What you see:**
- **Seed list:** how many of each crop you have (red at your cap) and each crop's grow time with your perks, lined
  up in columns ("Rice (4422) | 4,139 / 5,078 | 2d"). On phones the phone's own picker can't line them up.
- **A line under the seed menu:** about how many the planting gives with your perks and meals, how long it takes,
  your count against your cap (with a warning when some would go over), and the crop's mastery.
- **Grape Juice Vat:** the crop with the longest grow time that fits under your cap.
- **Feed Mill:** how much Feed one of each crop makes.

**Settings:**
- How many you have (in the seed list and on the line, red at your cap)
- What the planting gives, and the over-the-cap warning
- Grow times with your perks (in the seed list, and on the farm's line)
- Grape Juice Vat: the longest grow time that fits under your cap
- Feed Mill: how much Feed one of each crop makes, in its crop list
- Seed list order: the game's order, grow time, or seeds left

**Data:** the farm page itself (grow times), the Library (perks), your counts read when the page opens.

## Mining

**Where:** the Mines list, each mine and its location page.

**What you see:** each mine's next reward floor (and how many floors are left) and its Charms Active; on a mine, the
"Mining Floor Rewards" link says where the next rewards are.

**Settings:**
- Mines list: each mine's next rewards floor
- Mines list: each mine's Charms Active
- A mine and its location page: next rewards on the "Mining Floor Rewards" link

**Data:** reward floors from the Library; floors and charms from the pages you open.

## Unlock Goals

**Where:** item pages, shops and Town, for account unlocks: the Swamp Puzzle's pieces, the Cursed Effigy, House of
Cards, Gary's Crushroom.

**What you see:** where each part comes from, with links, and your next step with your progress (which quest of a
questline you're on, whether a shop has it this week, what has to be unlocked first).

**Settings:**
- Full next steps (off: just where each part comes from, with links)
- On the goal's item page (e.g. Swamp Puzzle)
- On a part's item page (e.g. Piece 04)
- In shops (Borgen Mercantile, Borgen's Camp, Flea Market)
- In Town: what opens a place that's still locked (e.g. House of Cards)

**Data:** the Library, buddy.farm (quests) and the pages you open.

## Library Hints

**Where:** the Temple, the Farmhouse, the Post Office.

**What you see:** cards that only hint at a secret also say what it is and how far along you are:
- Temple: "Something Strange" (the Cursed Effigy)
- Farmhouse: where the locked PIES's Pie Machine comes from
- Post Office: the Secret Passwords you haven't entered

**Settings:** each of the three above.

**Data:** the Library and buddy.farm; which passwords you've entered, from your Password Log.

## Quiz Answers

**Where:** a Schoolhouse quiz, while you take it.

**What you see:** a card under the answer buttons with the right answer. It starts folded on each question; tap
"Answer" to open it, and the right button gets the Toolkit's outline too. A question whose buttons don't match the
data gets no answer rather than a wrong one. It never answers for you.

**Settings:** whether the answer starts folded on each question.

**Data:** buddy.farm's quiz answers, checked against a second source when the Toolkit is built.

---

## Wishing Well

**Where:** the Wishing Well.

**What you see:** under "Select Item", what one toss of the item you picked can give and the chance of each, with
links to those items. It changes as you pick another item. It never tosses anything. Under the game's top text,
"Library:" links to every Library page about the Wishing Well: Wishing Well, WW Wants, Wishing Well Curios, Wishing
Well Tips, Murky WW and WW Drops Table (the list comes from the Library's own index when the Toolkit is built).

**Data:** buddy.farm, checked against the Library's WW Drops Table when the Toolkit is built.

## Exploring Counts

**Where:** an exploring place's screen (the one with Continue), under the game's buttons.

**What you see:** everything that place gives, in the game's own order, with your count "have / cap", red at your
cap. Each item links to its page. Your counts are read a few seconds after your last explore, so they're always the
game's own.

**Settings:**
- Layout on a wide screen: Tiles (picture, name, count) or List (two columns)
- Layout on a phone: Pictures (picture and count; names in tooltips) or List

**Data:** the place's own info page ("Things found here"), read when you open it; in the background only the first
time you visit a place, at the start of a new month, or when you find something not on its list (an event item).
Your counts: your inventory, read when you open the place and a few seconds after you stop exploring.

## buddy.farm Links

**Where:** wherever the Toolkit names a place, Help Request, pet or townsfolk (Item Uses, Friendship Gifts, the
Flea Market, Unlock Goals), and at the bottom of a Help Request, a place's info page, a pet's page and a townsfolk's
mailbox.

**What you see:** a small arrow after each name that opens its buddy.farm page in a new tab; the name itself still
opens the game's page. At the bottom of the pages above: "More: Library · buddy.farm" (the Library when it has a
page for it). Items already have both: their page's card ends in the same line, and every item the Toolkit names
links to that page.

**Settings:**
- A small arrow after each place, Help Request, pet and townsfolk the Toolkit names
- "More: Library · buddy.farm" at the bottom of their own pages

**Data:** buddy.farm's page addresses and the Library's page names, from their copies when the Toolkit is built.
Links only: nothing is read from buddy.farm for this.

## Toolkit Menu

**Where:** the Toolkit bar (the compass) at the bottom of the game's left menu. On phones, open the left menu.

**What you see:**
- **Data check:** pages to open so the Toolkit's numbers are current (your perks, Mastery, Help Requests, Townsfolk
  of the Day...), with links. The bar shows how many.
- **Shortcuts:** game pages you choose. A shop that's closed right now is greyed with "Closed"; it still opens.
- **Settings** and **Debug** (a report to copy when something looks wrong).

**Settings:**
- Data check
- Row height in the menu, on a wide screen: 44px (the game's size), 40px, 36px, or 32px with smaller text
  (starts at 40px)
- Row height in the menu, on a phone: the same choices (starts at 44px)
- Shortcuts: Mastery *(off)*, Help Requests, Friendship Levels *(off)*, Temple of Reward *(off)*, Locksmith *(off)*,
  Flea Market *(off)*, Borgen's Camp *(off)*, Borgen Mercantile *(off)*
- Toolkit: Settings, Debug (on while the Toolkit is being tested)

## On phones

- **Tooltips:** tap or hold the Toolkit's dotted text to see its tooltip; hold a link or button that has one. Tap again,
  tap elsewhere or scroll to close it.
- **Layout:** nothing the Toolkit adds runs off the screen. Item cards list one use or source per line.
- **Folding:** sections you fold (item cards, Friendship rows) stay folded, on every page.
